StudyBuddy case study

From finding a study partner to planning the week

StudyBuddy brings the tasks students do before and after a study session into a single web app, with separate tools for students and administrators.

Study partner recommendations

The matching service blends content-based scoring with collaborative filtering. It considers shared subjects and VARK questionnaire results, and offers a nearby filter. Students can send and respond to buddy requests.

Academic planning

Students can track tasks and deadlines, manage personal calendar events and export class timetables as PDF, Excel or calendar files.

Study and communication tools

Students can exchange direct messages and share study materials. The app also includes an AI study coach and a study-question generator.

Administration

Administrators can manage students, subjects and academic calendars, moderate uploaded materials and view reports.
